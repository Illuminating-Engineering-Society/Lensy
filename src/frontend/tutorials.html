Frequently asked questions

What is IES Lens?

An AI-assisted guide to navigate IES resources. Ask IES Lens to find standards excerpts, references, definitions, illuminance recommendations, and more. It interprets a question written in everyday language and links directly to the most relevant excerpts and to the source within the Lighting Library.

What is the IES Lighting Library?

The complete library of IES standards and guidelines. Subscriptions and leases come together in one browser-based collection, available on two personal devices: view, bookmark, and annotate standards on a computer, tablet, or phone, with offline browser access when needed.

Where do the answers come from?

IES Lens searches exclusively within IES technical content. Results display as referenced excerpts and link directly to the relevant pages of IES standards, so the source and its full context can always be reviewed.

Is the AI optional?

Yes. IES Lens is included with a Lighting Library subscription at no additional cost. The AI-assisted semantic search adds curation, and the AI Guide can be disabled from the profile menu to use IES Lens for keyword search only.

How can standards be purchased?

Two ways: subscribe to the complete Lighting Library (with IES Lens), or lease individual documents for three years. Both use the same browser-based tools (bookmarks and annotations) and support offline browser access.

Can IES Lens show what changed between editions?

Yes. Compare Versions explores differences between editions, including summaries of changes and additions. Subscribers receive access to deprecated standards for historical reference.

Where can I learn more?

IES eLearning is the education hub for IES expertise: in-depth courses, archived webinars and symposia, and more. For technical support, please complete the IES support form.
